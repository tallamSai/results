W01-An employee onboarding workflow where HR Representative collects new hire information including full name, email, phone number, department, job title, start date, and salary. The Hiring Manager reviews the submission and approves or rejects. If approved, IT Administrator provisions system access by entering username, email account, and access level. Simultaneously, Finance Officer sets up payroll with bank account details and tax information. Finally, HR Representative sends welcome package confirmation.

W02-A leave request workflow where Employee submits a leave request with leave type (annual, sick, personal), start date, end date, and reason. The Direct Manager reviews and approves or rejects the request. If rejected, Employee is notified. If approved, HR Representative updates the leave balance and records the approved leave in the system.

W03-A performance review workflow where HR Representative initiates the review cycle by selecting the employee and review period. The Direct Manager completes the performance evaluation form with ratings for goals achieved, teamwork, communication, and overall performance, plus written comments. The Department Head reviews and approves the evaluation. Finally, HR Representative schedules a feedback meeting and closes the review.

W04-An employee offboarding workflow where HR Representative initiates offboarding by entering employee name, last working day, and exit reason. The Direct Manager confirms pending work handover status. IT Administrator revokes system access and collects equipment including laptop, access card, and phone. Finance Officer processes final settlement with pending salary, leave encashment, and deductions. HR Representative conducts exit interview and archives employee records.

W05-A training request workflow where Employee submits a training request with course name, training provider, cost, duration, and business justification. The Direct Manager reviews relevance and approves or rejects. If approved, HR Representative checks budget availability. If budget available, Finance Officer processes payment. HR Representative then enrolls the employee and sends confirmation with training details.

W06-An expense reimbursement workflow where Employee submits expense claim with expense type, amount, date, description, and receipt upload. The Direct Manager verifies and approves or rejects. If approved, Finance Officer reviews for policy compliance and processes payment by entering payment date and transaction reference. Employee receives reimbursement confirmation.

W07-A purchase order workflow where Requester creates a purchase request with item description, quantity, estimated cost, vendor name, and urgency level. The Department Manager approves or rejects based on budget. If amount exceeds 10000, Finance Director provides additional approval. Procurement Officer creates the purchase order with PO number and delivery date. Upon delivery, Requester confirms receipt of goods.

W08-An invoice processing workflow where Accounts Payable Clerk receives and logs invoice with vendor name, invoice number, amount, due date, and line items. The Department Manager verifies goods or services received and approves. Finance Manager reviews for budget allocation. Accounts Payable Clerk schedules payment and records transaction. Vendor receives payment confirmation.

W09-A budget approval workflow where Department Manager submits annual budget request with department name, fiscal year, requested amount, and itemized breakdown. Finance Analyst reviews and provides recommendations. Finance Director approves, requests modifications, or rejects. If modifications requested, Department Manager revises and resubmits. Upon approval, Finance Analyst allocates budget in the system.

W10-A travel request workflow where Employee submits travel request with destination, travel dates, purpose, estimated airfare, hotel cost, and daily allowance. The Direct Manager approves or rejects. If international travel, Compliance Officer reviews visa and insurance requirements. Finance Officer verifies budget and issues travel advance. Upon return, Employee submits actual expenses for reconciliation.

W11-A system access request workflow where Employee requests system access with system name, access type (read, write, admin), business justification, and duration. The Direct Manager approves the business need. System Owner approves the specific access level. IT Administrator provisions access and sends credentials. After 30 days, System Owner reviews if access is still required.

W12-An incident management workflow where End User reports incident with title, description, severity (low, medium, high, critical), and affected system. Service Desk Agent triages and assigns priority. IT Technician investigates and implements fix. If escalation needed, IT Manager provides guidance. IT Technician resolves and documents solution. End User confirms resolution and closes ticket.

W13-A change request workflow where IT Staff submits change request with change title, description, affected systems, implementation plan, rollback plan, and proposed date. Change Manager reviews completeness. Change Advisory Board approves or rejects based on risk assessment. If approved, IT Staff implements change. IT Staff verifies success and closes the change request.

W14-A software license request workflow where Employee requests software with software name, version, business justification, and number of licenses. The Direct Manager approves business need. IT Procurement checks existing licenses and vendor options. Finance Officer approves cost. IT Administrator installs software and assigns license. Employee confirms software is working.

W15-A vendor onboarding workflow where Procurement Officer initiates vendor registration with company name, contact person, email, phone, address, and services offered. Compliance Officer reviews legal documents including business license and insurance certificates. Finance Officer sets up vendor in payment system with bank details and payment terms. Procurement Manager gives final approval. Vendor receives confirmation and welcome package.

W16-A contract approval workflow where Contract Manager drafts contract with vendor name, contract type, value, duration, and key terms. Legal Counsel reviews and suggests modifications. If value exceeds 50000, Executive Director approval is required. Finance Director confirms budget availability. Contract Manager finalizes and sends for vendor signature. Upon signed contract return, Contract Manager archives and activates the contract.

W17-An RFQ workflow where Procurement Officer creates RFQ with item specifications, quantity, delivery requirements, and deadline for responses. Procurement Officer sends RFQ to approved vendors. Upon receiving quotes, Procurement Analyst evaluates based on price, quality, and delivery time. Procurement Manager selects winning vendor. Procurement Officer notifies all vendors of decision and initiates purchase order for selected vendor.

W18-A facility maintenance workflow where Employee submits maintenance request with location, issue type (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, structural), description, and urgency. Facilities Coordinator reviews and assigns priority. Maintenance Technician inspects and provides repair estimate. If cost exceeds 500, Facilities Manager approves. Maintenance Technician completes repair and documents work done. Employee confirms issue resolved.

W19-A visitor access workflow where Employee requests visitor access with visitor name, company, visit date, visit purpose, and areas to access. The Direct Manager approves the visit. Security Officer runs background check for sensitive areas. Reception Desk prepares visitor badge. On visit day, Security Officer logs visitor entry and assigns escort if required. Upon departure, Security Officer logs exit and collects badge.

W20-A document approval workflow where Author creates document with title, type (policy, procedure, guideline), department, and content. Reviewer checks accuracy and provides feedback. If revisions needed, Author updates document. Department Head approves content. Quality Assurance verifies format compliance. Document Controller publishes document and notifies stakeholders. Document Controller archives previous version.
